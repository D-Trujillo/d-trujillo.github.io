I am a PhD student at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), working with [Mengjia Yan](https://people.csail.mit.edu/mengjia/). My research focuses on **microarchitectural security**: I study how performance optimizations in modern CPUs give rise to security vulnerabilities, and how we can defend against them without giving up performance.
On the offensive side, my work has uncovered new classes of transient execution attacks--including [Inception](https://comsec.ethz.ch/research/microarch/inception/) (CVE-2023-20569) and [Phantom](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3613424.3614275) (CVE-2022-23825)--leading to new mitigations in AMD processors. On the defensive side, I design principled, low-overhead mitigations against these attacks, often in close collaboration with industry.

During my PhD, I have interned at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/), [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/), and [AMD](https://www.amd.com/). I previously completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/), where I worked in the [COMSEC group](https://comsec.ethz.ch/) and received the ETH Medal for my thesis, and my B.Sc. in Computer Science at VU Amsterdam. My research is supported by an NDSEG Fellowship.
